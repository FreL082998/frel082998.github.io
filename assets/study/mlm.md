# MLM Membership and Binary Genealogy Management Platform

**Industry:** Direct Selling / Membership Management
**Role:** Full-Stack Developer
**Project Type:** Membership, Binary Genealogy, Bonus, Payout, and Content Management Platform
**Primary Technologies:** PHP, CodeIgniter, Bootstrap, JavaScript, jQuery, MySQL, Cloudflare, LAMP

## Project Overview

This project is a web-based membership and network-management platform designed to manage members, multiple membership accounts, binary genealogy structures, activation codes, pair-based bonuses, payout requests, notifications, and administrative operations.

The platform supports both administrators and registered members through separate operational workflows.

Members can maintain one or more accounts within a binary network, monitor their genealogy, request account codes, track pairing activity, and submit payout requests.

Administrators can manage membership records, approve code requests, monitor the binary hierarchy, process payouts, and review detailed system activity.

The system supports two network/account classifications identified in the platform as:

* **Tiger**
* **Eagle**

Both network types use left/right binary placement structures, while their pairing and bonus records are maintained independently.

### Business Value

The platform centralizes complex membership and network operations that would otherwise require significant manual tracking.

It provides structured management for:

* Member registration
* Multiple member accounts
* Binary genealogy
* Left/right placement
* Account activation codes
* Pair qualification
* Bonus records
* Payout requests
* Payout approval
* Member notifications
* Administrative activity monitoring

---

## The Challenge

Binary-network membership systems involve more than maintaining a simple list of users.

Individual members can own multiple network accounts, and each account can be positioned under another account on either the left or right side of a binary hierarchy.

As the network grows, several operational problems become difficult to manage manually:

* Determining account placement
* Maintaining parent-child relationships
* Tracking left and right branches
* Identifying completed account pairs
* Recording bonuses generated by pair activity
* Managing different membership-plan structures
* Issuing activation or registration codes
* Handling requests for new codes
* Managing member payouts
* Tracking payout approval status
* Providing members with status notifications
* Maintaining member profiles
* Tracking administrative operations

The platform needed to maintain these interconnected processes while providing administrators and members with understandable interfaces for managing their respective workflows.

I developed a centralized web application using PHP and CodeIgniter with a MySQL relational database.

# Multiple Account Management

A major feature of the platform is the ability for a single member to own multiple network accounts.

Member identity and network-account identity are separated.

This allows the relationship:

```text
Member
  ├── Account 1
  ├── Account 2
  ├── Account 3
  └── Additional Accounts
```

Each network account maintains its own:

* Account number
* Account owner
* Left-child account
* Right-child account
* Creation date

This enables a member to participate at multiple positions within the network without requiring a completely separate user profile for every position.

---

# Binary Genealogy Management

The platform maintains a binary genealogy for member accounts.

Each account can have:

* One left account
* One right account

Those accounts can then have their own left and right children, creating a recursive network structure.

```text
                  Account
                 /       \
              Left       Right
             /   \       /   \
           L1    L2    R1    R2
          / \    / \   / \    / \
         ...    ...   ...      ...
```

The application activity records show dedicated functionality for viewing the **Binary Genealogy** of individual member accounts.

Users can navigate through different account numbers to inspect their corresponding network branches.

---

# Binary Account Placement

New network accounts can be positioned beneath existing accounts.

The placement process connects the new account to either the:

* Left branch
* Right branch

of another account.

The application records placement activities such as accounts being paired beneath specific parent accounts, demonstrating that genealogy placement is an active operational workflow rather than simply a reporting view.

This structure allows the network hierarchy to grow dynamically as members add new accounts.

# Tiger Network

The primary binary-account structure is represented by the platform's standard account and pair records.

Tiger codes are used within this network type.

Tiger accounts maintain:

* Member ownership
* Left account
* Right account
* Account creation date

When qualifying left and right accounts form a pair, the system stores the pair relationship and associated bonus.

The database contains Tiger pair records with a recorded bonus of **1,000** for qualified pairs.

The application therefore separates:

```text
Account Structure
        ↓
Left / Right Placement
        ↓
Qualified Pair
        ↓
Pair Record
        ↓
Bonus
```

# Pair and Bonus Management

The platform records successful left/right account pairs.

A pair record contains:

* Parent account
* Qualified left account
* Qualified right account
* Bonus amount

This creates a historical record of the pairings that contributed to member earnings.

### Pairing Workflow

```text
Parent Account
      ↓
Left Account Added
      +
Right Account Added
      ↓
Pair Qualification
      ↓
Pair Record Created
      ↓
Bonus Assigned
      ↓
Member Earnings Updated
```

Separate pairing tables are maintained for Tiger and Eagle accounts.

This keeps compensation-related records distinct from the genealogy structure itself.

Administrator Notified
        ↓

        ↓
